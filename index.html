David Meldofsky

California-licensed attorney. Editor and publisher.

David Meldofsky writes and publishes about lawsuits, litigation, consumer rights, and the business of plaintiff-side law in the age of AI from California. He founded and edits three independent properties, each serving a different audience.

Lawsuit Intelligencerlawsuitintelligencer.com

Editorial analysis of mass torts, product liability, toxic exposure litigation, and the business of plaintiff-side law in the age of AI. Written for attorneys, journalists, and firms working inside the litigation industry.

Lawsuit Informerlawsuitinformer.com

Plain-English public legal education on lawsuits, consumer safety, and legal rights. Written for the general reader trying to understand how a particular kind of litigation works.

Lawsuit Centerlawsuit.center

A searchable directory and case-review hub connecting consumers with plaintiff-side firms across mass tort, product liability, and consumer protection categories.
